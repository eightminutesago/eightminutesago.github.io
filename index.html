Eight Minutes Ago
Real images · Old light · Daily

The sunlight you see left the Sun eight minutes ago.

Eight Minutes Ago is a space and science channel. Every short video is built from real imagery published by NASA, ESA and the James Webb and Hubble telescope teams, explained in under a minute and fact-checked against its sources. No AI-generated space footage, ever. Every image is credited on screen and in the description.

Where to watch

New videos most days on YouTube Shorts, plus TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels.

About the tools behind it

Eight Minutes Ago runs its own small publishing tool. It prepares videos from public-domain and openly licensed space imagery, and, with the channel owner's approval, uploads them to the channel's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It is used only by the channel owner and only for these accounts.

Imagery courtesy of NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI and mission teams. Eight Minutes Ago is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or any space agency.

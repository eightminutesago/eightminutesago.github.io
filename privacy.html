Eight Minutes Ago

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

This policy covers the Eight Minutes Ago website and the publishing tool the channel owner uses to manage the Eight Minutes Ago accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook (the "Tool").

Who uses the Tool

The Tool is used only by the owner of Eight Minutes Ago to publish to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data about viewers or other users.

What data the Tool accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploads videos and their titles, descriptions and schedules to the owner's channel, and reads that channel's own analytics (views, watch time, likes, subscribers gained).
  • TikTok: basic profile information of the owner's account and the ability to publish the owner's videos to that account.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): publishes Reels to the owner's Instagram professional account and Facebook Page.

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to anyone except the platform they belong to, and the Tool does not use them for advertising.

Google and YouTube

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google security settings.

Website visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address as part of serving them.

Deleting data

To remove the Tool's access, revoke it in your Google, TikTok or Meta account settings; the Tool then deletes the stored token on its next run. Questions: austinmacadam238@gmail.com.
